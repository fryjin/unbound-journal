# Repository Naming

## Selected repository codename

`unbound-journal`

Rationale:

- `Unbound` already exists in the broader brand/project history and communicates freedom of creation well.
- `journal` clearly describes the product domain for engineering and deployment.
- The repository codename does not force the final consumer-facing product name to remain the same.

## Alternatives retained

- `unbound-paper` — stronger Paper Engine emphasis, but narrower than the eventual product.
- `unbound-notebook` — physical notebook feeling, but less natural for the broader journal category.
- `paperbound` — distinctive, but conflicts semantically with the “unbound” creative idea.
- `xlp-journal` — historical continuity, but XLP is too closely associated with the music product line.

Final product naming remains open and is not a P0 blocker.
